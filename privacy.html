2. Data We Collect

None. Partyfold does not collect any personal data.

The app:

  • Does not require an account or login
  • Does not send any of your data to us or to third parties (the only network traffic is Apple's App Store checking your subscription)
  • Does not use analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry
  • Does not use advertising or ad tracking
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, or any other device sensors
  • Does not use cookies

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Partyfold stores your settings and game progress locally on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app

4. Subscription and Third-Party Services

Partyfold offers an auto-renewing weekly subscription. Purchase, payment, renewal and cancellation are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID; Apple only tells the app on your device whether a subscription is active. Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID account settings.

Partyfold does not integrate any other third-party services, SDKs, frameworks, or libraries that collect data. There are no ads, no analytics providers, and no social media integrations.

5. Children's Privacy

Partyfold does not collect any data from any user, including children.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Since we do not collect or process any personal data, there is no data for us to provide, correct, delete, or restrict. If you believe we have inadvertently collected any data, please contact us and we will promptly address your concern.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU Member State of your habitual residence.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices (for example, by adding online features), we will update this privacy policy accordingly. The current version is always available at this URL.
