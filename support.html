Partyfold

How it works

Partyfold is a party pack with three games: Last Degree, Dice Cup and Impostor. Pass one phone around the group. On a foldable iPhone, the app uses both halves of the screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Partyfold work offline?

Yes. No internet connection is needed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Partyfold and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current week ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed but the games are locked. What now?

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. If that does not help, write to us.

Does Partyfold collect my data?

No. See our Privacy Policy for details.
